# Synthetic sales

Revenue is the sum of booked order amounts. Each order amount equals quantity multiplied by unit_price in USD. All orders are completed; the dataset contains no taxes, discounts, cancellations, or refunds.

This demonstration snapshot contains 480 synthetic orders for 80 synthetic customers across North and South, covering 2025-01-01 through 2025-04-30. Products are Monitor, Keyboard, Dock, and Mouse. Customer identifiers do not represent real people.

The approved revenue tool supports an optional region filter. For date, product, customer, or quantity analysis, use custom SQL on the warehouse source and orders table.
